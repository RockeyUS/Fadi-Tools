## Fadi Tools

Fadi Tools is an all-in-one Windows app: convert files, compress videos and pictures, edit photos and videos, work with PDFs, inspect files and download from the internet. It works offline, it's free, and it's in English and Arabic.

**Download:** get **Fadi.Tools.zip** (everything included), unzip it and open **Fadi Tools.exe**. Or get **Fadi.Tools.exe** on its own; it downloads its engines (about 50 MB) the first time.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new in 1.2.0

### Added
- **Edit every page of a PDF in Studio.** Use the ‹ › arrows while editing to move between pages; each page keeps its edits, and **Save as PDF** writes all pages into one file.
- **Grow smaller files to this size** (Compress). Turn it on to make pictures, videos and songs *bigger*, up to the size you set, for sites that ask for a minimum size.
- **Light / dark button** at the top-left corner of the window.
- **Night logo**: the snake in white on black, with the red fez.

### Improved
- **Clearer light mode**: stronger borders, darker secondary text, and cards that stand out from the background.
- **A line under the top bar** separates it from the rest of the app.
- **Previews are on by default** in Convert and Compress.

### Fixed
- Changing the language or theme made the whole Windows desktop refresh. Now only the app changes.

### Removed
- The "My picture" logo option.
