## Fadi Tools

Fadi Tools is an all-in-one Windows app: convert files, compress videos and pictures, edit photos, videos and PDFs, rename files, find duplicates, inspect files and download from the internet. It works offline, it's free, and it's in English and Arabic.

**Download:** get **Fadi.Tools.zip** (everything included), unzip it and open **Fadi Tools.exe**. Or get **Fadi.Tools.exe** on its own; it downloads its engines the first time.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new in 1.6.2

### Added
- **Crop on the Donut Wheel.** Pictures have a new **Crop** action in the middle ring. Dropping a picture on it opens a small Crop window of its own; Fadi Tools itself doesn't open. It shows the picture with a frame: drag the frame to move it, drag its corners and edges to resize it, or draw a new one. The rest of the picture is dimmed, and thirds lines show while you drag. **Aspect ratio** snaps the frame to Free, Original, 1:1, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3 or 3:4. **Size** shows the frame in pixels, and you can type a width and height. **Reset** brings back the whole picture. **Apply** saves the cropped copy next to the original ("photo Cropped.jpg"), in the same format, and it shows in Activity. Wheels that were already customized get Crop added once, after Compress, if the middle ring has room.
- **Wheel size.** The Donut Wheel page has a **Size** slider (55–130%). The wheel beside it grows and shrinks smoothly as you move the slider, and it's shown at the size it will open on your desktop.

### Changed
- **Compress on the wheel opens its own small window** instead of working in the background. It shows the file and its size, **How much smaller** (Light, Balanced or Strong) with a line about each one, and an optional **target size in MB**. Then it compresses right there with a progress bar and shows the result ("614 KB → 112 KB · Saved 502 KB (82% smaller)"), with **Show file**. If the file can't get smaller, it says so and keeps the original as it is. The level you pick is remembered for next time. Fadi Tools itself stays closed.
- **The Donut Wheel is a little smaller to start with** (80% of its old size). Use the new Size slider to change it.
- **The panel by the clock is smaller and tighter**, with shorter labels: **Open** instead of "Open Fadi Tools", and **Download copied links**.

### Fixed
- Dragging a file from near a corner or an edge of the screen opened the Donut Wheel half off the screen. The wheel now always opens fully on the screen, moved in from the edge when needed (it also keeps clear of the taskbar).
