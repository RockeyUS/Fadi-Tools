## Fadi Tools

Fadi Tools is an all-in-one Windows app: convert files, compress videos and pictures, edit photos, videos and PDFs, rename files, find duplicates, inspect files and download from the internet. It works offline, it's free, and it's in English and Arabic.

**Download:** get **Fadi.Tools.zip** (everything included), unzip it and open **Fadi Tools.exe**. Or get **Fadi.Tools.exe** on its own; it downloads its engines the first time.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new in 1.6.4

A design update: easier to read, more consistent from page to page, and more of its own look.

### Changed
- **Easier to read.**
  - The words on orange buttons (Convert, Download, the picked format…) were too faint: white on that orange was 2.7:1. The buttons now use a deeper orange, so white reads at 5:1.
  - Small grey hints and counts (file counts, "← smaller / bigger →", "Saves to Downloads"…) were 3.9:1 and are now 5.8:1 in the dark look and 5.4:1 in the light one.
  - In the light look, orange text and lines are a little deeper too.
- **A calmer light look.** The cream background is now a soft warm grey, with matching lines and borders. The dark look stays as it was.
- **Titles in Windows 11's own type** (Segoe UI Variable Display) instead of the book serif. Arabic stays in its own font. The big size numbers in Compress keep the serif.
- **A ring marks where you are.** The page you're on has a small orange ring at the end of its row in the sidebar, the shape of the Donut Wheel. While work runs, Activity shows a ring that fills as it goes, with how many tasks are running inside it.
- **Empty pages show what they do.** The dashed drop area is now a frame in the middle with the page's icon in a ring (it sweeps round when the mouse comes near), and the whole page still takes drops.
  - **Rename** shows an example: IMG_2041.jpg → Trip 001.jpg.
  - **Duplicates** has one-click buttons to look in Downloads, Pictures or the Desktop.
  - **File inspector** and **Studio** list the last files you opened there.
  - The arrow keys pick these buttons and Enter opens them.
  - Each page's tip at the bottom is about that page now.
- **The Studio has three tabs: Photos, PDF and Videos.** The PDF tools (combine files, edit pages, sign, lock…) are the PDF tab, so the "PDF tools" button is gone. **Close** closes the open PDF and stays on the PDF tab.
- **Compress has its button at the bottom of the options**, like Convert, with **Show file** beside it when it's done. The separate Actions card is gone; Add files and Clear are at the top of the file list.
- **Downloader:** the button that looks a link up is called **Look up** (it said "Get video" even for GIFs). Once a link is found, it turns plain, so **Download** is the one orange button.
- **The Convert panel is clearer.**
  - The picked format is outlined in orange instead of filled, so the Convert button is the one solid orange thing.
  - Once files are added, a card shows what will happen: "2 files · 688 KB" over a big **JPG → PNG** (in Arabic it reads right to left).
  - With no files, the button says **Add files to convert** instead of a grey "Convert".
  - **Change** (next to the save folder) looks like a button, and a long folder path is shortened in the middle, so its end stays visible.
  - The quality choices say **Best quality · Balanced · Smaller file**.
  - JPEG is gone from the picture formats; it's the same as JPG.
- **Settings → Windows integration** is in three groups: Shortcuts and File Explorer, Notifications, and Running in the background.
