## Fadi Tools

Fadi Tools is an all-in-one Windows app: convert files, compress videos and pictures, edit photos, videos and PDFs, rename files, find duplicates, inspect files and download from the internet. It works offline, it's free, and it's in English and Arabic.

**Download:** get **Fadi.Tools.zip** (everything included), unzip it and open **Fadi Tools.exe**. Or get **Fadi.Tools.exe** on its own; it downloads its engines the first time.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new in 1.5.1

### Added
- **Tabs in the Studio.** Each photo or PDF you open on the Photos / PDF side gets its own tab, so you can work on two or more at once. Dropping or opening another file adds a tab instead of closing the one you were working on. Switch by clicking a tab, close one with its × (or a middle click, or Ctrl+W), and start another with + (or Ctrl+T). A dot on a tab means it has unsaved changes, and closing it asks first.
- **Right-click menu for pictures on a page.** Right-click a picture you added on top of a PDF page or photo for: **Light & color…** (its own light, contrast, color and warmth, changing as you drag), **Replace picture…**, **Turn right**, **Flip (mirror)**, **Back to its own shape**, **Duplicate** (Ctrl+D), **Bring to front**, **Send to back** and **Remove** (Del). Text has its own menu (edit, duplicate, front/back, remove), an empty spot offers Add text / Add photo, and outside editing the menu has Edit, Copy text, turn, Convert and Show in folder. The menu grows out of the mouse and shrinks away when it closes.
- **A clear (×) button in the Download link box.**
- **Fadi Tools by the clock.** The app now has an icon in the system tray (also under "Show hidden icons") for as long as it's open: click it to bring the window back, right-click → Exit. It stays there while it keeps working after the window is closed. Settings → "Show Fadi Tools by the clock" turns it off.

### Improved
- **Copy text in PDFs.** The page is drawn again at 300 dpi just for reading, so small print reads much better. A page turned in the viewer is now read both ways round and the read with real words wins, so turned pages no longer come out as nonsense. Searchable PDFs are read sharper too.
- **Live Adjust.** Light, contrast, color, warmth, blur and sharpness show the result while the slider moves (a quick preview), and the full-quality picture a moment after it stops.
- **Live stretching.** Stretching a picture from its sides follows the mouse instead of jumping into place after you let go.
- **File inspector.** A file you drop or open is ready in both **File details** and **Reverse engineer**. **Analyze** now runs every kind of analysis that fits the file (overview, .NET assembly, classes and methods, native calls, icons & resources) one after another; pick any of them above to see its result without analyzing again.
- The video project menu (and every small menu) now grows out of where it was opened and shrinks back when it closes, and works with the arrow keys and Enter.

### Fixed
- Dropping a new file in the Studio closed the one you were working on.
- In Download, removing the link (Ctrl+A, Delete) left the old video showing.
