## Fadi Tools

Fadi Tools is an all-in-one Windows app: convert files, compress videos and pictures, edit photos and videos, work with PDFs, rename files, inspect files and download from the internet. It works offline, it's free, and it's in English and Arabic.

**Download:** get **Fadi.Tools.zip** (everything included), unzip it and open **Fadi Tools.exe**. Or get **Fadi.Tools.exe** on its own; it downloads its engines (about 50 MB) the first time.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new in 1.3.0

### Added
- **Page strip in Studio.** When you edit a PDF, all its pages show along the bottom. Click any page to jump to it; edited pages have a dot.
- **Adjust photos (Studio).** A new Adjust tab with looks (Vivid, Warm, Cool, Black & white, Sepia, Vintage, Faded, Dramatic), light, contrast, color, warmth, blur and sharpness. Hold **Hold to compare** to see the original.
- **Remove a video's background (Studio → Effects).** Make it see-through, a color, blurred, or a picture. It's done frame by frame on your PC by the same AI as photos, and shows the time left in Activity.
- **Stickers (Convert → Sticker).** Turn pictures, GIFs and videos into WhatsApp, Telegram and Discord stickers or Discord emoji, at the right size and under each app's file-size limit.
- **PDF: sign, number, watermark and lock (PDF tools → Edit pages).** Draw your signature (or use a picture of it) and drag it where it goes; add page numbers; add a watermark; add a password. Locked PDFs now open after you type their password, and you can save a copy without it.
- **Rename page.** Rename many files at once: numbered, find & replace, text added at the start or end, or capital letters changed. You see every new name before anything changes, and **Undo rename** puts the old names back.
- **Check online (File inspector).** Asks 70+ antivirus programs on VirusTotal about a file, using your own free key (Settings or the gear next to the button). Only the file's fingerprint is sent; the file is uploaded only if VirusTotal has never seen it and you agree.
- **Drag files out of the app.** Drag a finished file from Convert, Compress, Download or Rename (or the photo in Studio) straight into Discord, WhatsApp, an email or a folder.
- **What's new window** after an update (also in Settings and in the search).
- **Tips** on empty pages, a different one each time.
- **Animations setting** in Settings → Appearance, to turn the motion off.

### Improved
- **More animation:** light/dark mode switches with a circle that grows from the button, language changes fade, tabs slide to the one you pick and their content fades in, buttons and the side list ripple when clicked, and the selection glides between sections.
- **Language button** now sits next to the light/dark button at the top; **Settings** moved to the bottom of the side list, under Activity.
- **Ctrl+V works everywhere:** files, copied pictures, links (on the Download page) and paths copied with "Copy as path".
- **Each page remembers the folder** you last opened files from.
- **Undo and redo** for photos moved to the top bar in Studio, so the editing tabs have room.
- The Settings page scrolls when the window is short.

### Fixed
- Switching PDF pages in Studio with the arrow keys made the whole page flash and reload. It now changes smoothly inside the editor.
- Pressing the arrow keys quickly while editing a PDF put a copy of the text box on every page.
