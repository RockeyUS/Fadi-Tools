## Fadi Tools

Fadi Tools is an all-in-one Windows app: convert files, compress videos and pictures, edit photos, videos and PDFs, rename files, find duplicates, inspect files and download from the internet. It works offline, it's free, and it's in English and Arabic.

**Download:** get **Fadi.Tools.zip** (everything included), unzip it and open **Fadi Tools.exe**. Or get **Fadi.Tools.exe** on its own; it downloads its engines the first time.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new in 1.6.1

### Added
- **Donut Wheel Customization.** Settings has a new **Donut Wheel Customization** card (above the version and uninstall card). It opens a page with a tab for each kind of file: Images, Videos, Audio, PDF, Documents and Data. For each one, click formats to put them on the outer ring (up to 8) and actions to put them in the middle ring. For example, PDFs can now offer **PowerPoint** (PPTX), ODT, RTF, EPUB, JPG and more. A live wheel beside the choices shows the result: point at its slices to try it, or drag a slice onto another to move it there. The numbers on the chosen formats show their place on the wheel, starting at the top. **Reset to default** brings back the usual set for that kind of file. The page also has the wheel's on/off switch.
- **Quick switches by the clock.** Right-clicking the Fadi Tools icon by the clock opens a small panel instead of a plain menu. It shows whether Fadi Tools is working on something and has switches for the **Donut Wheel**, **Keep running when closed**, **Offer to download copied links** and **Notifications**. It also has **Customize wheel**, **Open Fadi Tools**, **Settings** and **Exit** buttons. It closes when you click anywhere else or press Esc.

### Changed
- **The drag + Shift wheel is now called the Donut Wheel**, in Settings and everywhere else in the app.
- **The app icon is dark by default.** The taskbar, title bar, Start menu and the icon by the clock use the dark logo (an orange ف on a charcoal tile). Choosing Settings → App logo → **Light** still switches the window and tray icon to the light one.
- **One Fadi Tools in the right-click menu.** Right-clicking a picture showed two Fadi Tools entries: the Fadi Tools menu and a separate "Copy text" item. **Copy text** (for pictures) and **View** (for the photo types Fadi Tools opens) are now inside the one Fadi Tools menu, next to Convert, Compress, Edit and Details. Existing installs update the menu by themselves the first time 1.6.1 starts.
- Turning the Donut Wheel on or off in one place (Settings, its own page or the panel by the clock) now updates the other switches straight away.

### Fixed
- The logo inside the app looked jagged around its edges, mostly with the Light logo. It's now drawn smoothly everywhere: the side list, Settings, What's new, the welcome tour and setup. The small sizes use the bolder drawing made for them.
- The Donut Wheel no longer offers conversions that can't be made, like PowerPoint from a plain text or Markdown file, or Markdown from a spreadsheet.
