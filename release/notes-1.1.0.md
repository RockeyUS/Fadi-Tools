## Fadi Tools

Fadi Tools is an all-in-one Windows app: convert files, compress videos and pictures, edit photos and videos, work with PDFs, inspect files and download from the internet. It works offline, it's free, and it's in English and Arabic.

**Download:** get **Fadi.Tools.zip** (everything included), unzip it and open **Fadi Tools.exe**. Or get **Fadi.Tools.exe** on its own; it downloads its engines (about 50 MB) the first time.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new in 1.1.0

### Added
- **Photos on top of photos, PDFs and videos (Studio).** "Add photo" puts another picture on top that you can move, resize, rotate, frame, shade and fade. You can also drop a photo onto the picture or video you're editing.
- **Rotate** for text and photos in Studio.
- **Outline colors and thickness** for text, and frame colors for photos.
- **Choose what to copy with Copy text.** Drag a box over just the part you want (Enter = the whole picture). In the result window, select some of the text to copy only that, or click "Read another part".
- **Previews in Convert and Compress.** Switch between List and Previews to see each photo, a frame of each video, the first page of each PDF, and Windows' own thumbnails for other files.

### Improved
- **Smoother text size slider.** Finer steps at small sizes, and the text follows your hand instantly while dragging (also when dragging a corner).
- **Compress presets** are now Discord, Email and Avatar (WhatsApp removed).
- The Studio tab is now called **Text & photos**.

### Fixed
- Clicking **Search** while the app was in Arabic showed an error ("Error creating window handle").
