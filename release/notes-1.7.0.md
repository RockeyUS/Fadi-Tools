## Fadi Tools

Fadi Tools is an all-in-one Windows app: convert files, compress videos and pictures, edit photos, videos and PDFs, rename files, find duplicates, inspect files and download from the internet. It works offline, it's free, and it's in English and Arabic.

**Download:** get **Fadi.Tools.zip** (everything included), unzip it and open **Fadi Tools.exe**. Or get **Fadi.Tools.exe** on its own; it downloads its engines the first time.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new in 1.7.0

The Studio is rebuilt from scratch. The photo side works like a small Photoshop and the video side like a small CapCut. Everything still runs on your PC: no account, no uploads, no cloud.

### New photo studio
- **Opening a photo starts editing it.** The workspace has tools down the left, the photo in the middle with rulers, and on the right the settings for what you picked, with the layers and history under them.
- **Layers.** Photos, text, painting and adjustments each sit on a layer. You can:
  - show or hide them, lock them and change their opacity;
  - use blend modes (Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Soft light, Color…);
  - drag them into a new order, or into groups (folders);
  - double-click a layer to rename it.
  - Dropping a picture on the photo adds it as a new layer.
- **Adjustment layers.** These change everything under them without touching the pixels, and the eraser takes them away from parts of the photo:
  - Light & color, with the filters;
  - Levels, with a chart of the photo's light;
  - Curves, a curve you drag;
  - Hue & saturation, for all colors or just one range such as the blues.
- **Selections.**
  - Tools: rectangle, oval, lasso and magic wand.
  - **Select subject** finds the person, pet or product with the background AI.
  - Shift adds to a selection, Alt takes away, and a soft-edge setting blurs its border.
  - With something selected: copy it to a new layer (Ctrl+J), fill it, clear it, crop to it, or add an adjustment for just that part.
- **Brush and eraser.**
  - Painting goes on a layer of its own.
  - On photo and adjustment layers the eraser only hides parts (it adds a mask), and Alt+erase brings them back.
- **Remove object (J).** Paint over a person, a sign or a stain and it's filled in from the photo around it. The fix goes on a "Retouch" layer you can hide or erase. It works on your PC; nothing is uploaded.
- **Move and place.**
  - Drag things on the photo with handles to move, size and turn them.
  - Smart guides snap to the photo's edges and middle, and to other layers.
  - Exact X, Y, W, H and angle boxes, six align buttons (to the photo or the selection), and flipping.
- **History.** You can undo about 40 steps (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y), or click any step in the History tab.
- **Projects.** Ctrl+S saves a photo project (.fphoto) that keeps every layer. Your edit is backed up every minute, and offered back if the app ever closes unexpectedly.
- **Export (Ctrl+Shift+E).** Choose JPG, PNG, WEBP or PDF, the quality and the size. It shows how big the file will be as you change them, and warns when a format can't keep see-through parts.
- **New canvas.** Start from ready-made sizes: square post, story/reel, video thumbnail, wallpaper, sticker (see-through) or A4 print. You can also type any size.
- **Also new:** crop with fixed shapes, image size, turning and flipping the whole photo with every layer, copy text from the photo or just the selected part, and copy and paste pictures.
- **Keyboard shortcuts for every tool:** V, M, L, W, B, E, J, T, C, I, H and Z. Space+drag moves around the photo. More → Keyboard shortcuts lists them all.

### New video studio
- **A timeline with tracks.** Text tracks are on top, then overlays (picture-in-picture), then the main video, then music and sounds. Captions have their own track.
  - Clips show small pictures of what's in them, and sounds show their wave.
  - Ctrl + mouse wheel zooms the timeline.
  - Clips snap to each other and to the playhead (you can turn the magnet off).
- **Editing.**
  - Split at the playhead (S), and trim by dragging a clip's ends or with I / O.
  - Drag clips to move them, to reorder the main video, or to another track of the same kind.
  - Deleting a clip from the main video closes the gap.
  - Duplicate, replace, and right-click for more.
- **Transitions for each cut.** There are 12, including cross fade, through black or white, slide, wipe, circle, zoom and blur, each with its own length.
- **Speed from 0.1× to 10×** for each clip. The sound keeps its pitch.
- **Keyframes (◆).** Make overlays and texts move, grow, turn and fade over time: set a keyframe, move the playhead, then change the setting or drag the overlay on the picture. Sound volume can have keyframes too, for example to bring the music down while someone speaks.
- **In and out animations** for texts and overlays: fade, slide up, slide in and pop. Texts can also type themselves out.
- **Sound.**
  - Add music on its own track.
  - Fade sounds in and out.
  - **Detach audio** moves a video's sound to its own track.
  - **Remove background noise** works on any clip.
- **Captions.**
  - Auto captions (on your PC, as before) now go on their own track.
  - Pick a look: Classic, Box, **Word highlight** (the word being said lights up) or **Bold pop**, with your own font, size, colors and position.
  - Fix any word in a simple list.
  - Import .srt subtitles, or save the captions as an .srt file.
- **Shape.** Choose Original, 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, 1:1, 4:5 or 4:3. Clips that don't fit get a blurred copy of themselves behind them, or black, or a color you pick.
- **Each clip has its own light & color and filters.**
- **Remove a video's background.** As before it runs on your PC. The cut-out now goes on an overlay track above the original clip, so you can hide or replace what's behind it.
- **Preview.** The picture under the playhead shows texts, overlays, keyframes and captions as you edit. Play (Space) shows the finished video, with J/K/L and frame-by-frame arrow keys.
- **Export (Ctrl+E).** Choose MP4, GIF or sound-only MP3, the size (up to 4K), 24–60 fps, a quality level and the graphics card. It shows roughly how big the file will be.
- **Projects** keep every track, keyframe and caption. Projects from earlier versions open in the new studio with their clips, texts, music and subtitles.

### Fixed
- **Watch folders:** once a file is converted, the original goes to the Recycle Bin, so only the new file is left. Compressed files keep their original.
- **Discord GIFs:** Fadi Tools no longer opens Edge. KLIPY (the GIFs Discord uses) only lets a person in a web browser open its GIF pages, so a klipy.com/gifs/… link now explains how to get the GIF's own link instead: open it in your browser, right-click, **Copy image address**, and paste that. Those links, and GIF links copied from Discord itself, download straight away.
- **Donut Wheel:** dragging a folder over it no longer flickers. Folders now get Convert all, Compress, Rename all and Duplicates.
- **Duplicates:** the Downloads, Pictures and Desktop buttons show their full names.
